# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

The Missing Hour is intended to be a welcoming project for players, contributors, and members of the community.

Participants are expected to:

* Be respectful
* Be constructive
* Discuss ideas rather than attack people
* Accept reasonable criticism
* Avoid harassment
* Avoid discriminatory or hateful behavior
* Respect different opinions and experiences

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not tolerated:

* Harassment
* Personal attacks
* Threats
* Hate speech
* Discriminatory remarks
* Deliberate disruption
* Publishing private information
* Abusive or inappropriate content

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to maintain a respectful community.

## Reporting

If you encounter unacceptable behavior, contact the project maintainers through the repository's available contact channels.

This code of conduct applies to all project spaces operated by The Missing Hour and FunLogic Studios.
